The pecha

Fly your line across a rival's line and the two hook — from above or below. Hooked lines saw each other until one is cut. What cuts is line moving through the crossing: a fast khainch or a long dheel drags your glass-coated line across one spot of theirs. A still line loses. Back out the way you came to escape. A kite that only rests its body on another line slides off it, and a pecha nobody works ends within a few seconds — the line that has taken the most wear parts. Upar ya neeche: from on top, dheel cuts best; from below, khainch cuts best — your card (top-left) tells you which you are. A quick tunka in a pecha is a jhatka: one sharp jerk that saws hard for a moment. A third kite can join a pecha that is already on — two lines on one (in a Mohalla, a team-mate coming to help) — but never more than three lines in one tangle. The ring at each crossing is green while you are cutting and red while you are being cut. The cut goes to whoever did the most of the cutting.

Flying

Point the nose with steer — a quick tap nudges it, holding turns it fast; it stays where you leave it — then hold Fly and the kite goes that way. Flying up when the kite is right overhead lets a little line slip so it climbs out again, and near the roofs a dive turns into a skim. Kite on the roof? Press Fly to launch it again. It runs out of power near the edges of the sky and overhead, like a real kite; let go of everything and it settles back into a hover. A quick tap is a tunka — the kite dips, swings and darts. On slack line a light patang spins by itself; tunka when the nose points where you want.

Loot & scoring

Cut kites fall trailing string — and in a Festival, kites cut on other rooftops keep drifting over yours. Lapetna: sweep your line through a falling kite or its dor and it wraps round your line; the kite then flies on your line. Khainch to bring it down to your hands before the street kids get it — they snag it with their jhaar sticks, or catch hold of the dor it trails and haul it down hand over hand (watch the Loot chase window). Sweep your line through it while they're pulling and you can still snatch it away. Cut +3 · kite home +2 · lose a kite −1 · cuts in a row earn bonus points.

Mohalla (team matches)

Your street against theirs, 2 vs 2 or 3 vs 3. Team-mates' strings never cut each other; the side with more cuts wins. Climb the career - six neighbourhoods, each one unlocked by beating the one before - or play a quick team match. Online rooms can be played in teams too.

Coins, shop & collection

Every match pays coins: 10 per cut, 8 per kite brought home, 40 for a win, plus the daily challenge and career rewards. Spend them in the Shop on kites, strings, reels and fliers - what you buy is yours for good. Every kite you bring home goes into your Collection, and the weekly Leaderboard shows the best cutters, streaks and looters.

Quick chat & replays

Press T (or the chat button) for quick phrases, or 1-8 to send one straight away. In a team, "Help me!" brings a team-mate. After a cut you were part of, a short slow-motion replay plays - skip it, or switch replays off in Settings.

Rank

Every cut, every kite you bring home and every win earns XP. Climb from Naya to Khalifa — your rank is shown on the main menu.

Match

Play to a clock, with no limit, or as a race: First to 5 or First to 10 cuts wins.

Wind & trophies

The hawa shifts during a match — watch the gauge under the timer. Your cuts, wins and streaks unlock Trophies on the main menu.
